One-page newspaper front page summing up the world's news of Friday 20 November 2026: 42 verified stories across 7 regions, three lead stories, a stylised world map with 12 numbered locations, eight illustrated briefs, a DATE CHECK box and a markets strip. Built to read at thumbnail, half and full size.

Entry for [Sum up 20 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261120) on make.fun.

## Run

```sh
python3 render.py news.png
python3 main.py
```

`render.py` draws `news.png` (1024x1024, Pillow only). `main.py` copies it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output/entry.png`). No network, deterministic.

## What's inside

- Masthead: Friday, 20 November 2026 — 42 stories, 7 regions.
- Leads: Asia's AI sell-off, COP31 dropping the fossil-fuel phase-out, Berlin on Ukraine talks.
- Map: 12 places in the news, with a legend.
- Panels: Nvidia probe, GTA 6's $2.3bn launch, Bukele's third-term bid, UK-EU youth mobility, Gaza's Yellow Line, Japan inflation, Sudan drone strike, IMF debt warning.
- The day in brief: all 42 stories, numbered.
- DATE CHECK: every story happened or was first reported on 20 November 2026; the big 19 November stories (ICJ ruling, Shenzhen summit, Anthropic IPO close) are named as left out.
- By the numbers: S&P 500, Nasdaq, Nikkei, Taiex, Kospi, SoftBank, gold, Brent, US 10-year yield.

Sources for every story: see [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md).
